Fork Calendar

A calendar for Mac and iPhone, built by CW&T for our own use. It talks to Google Calendar directly: your calendars and events are mirrored onto the device, drawn in a single monospace grid, and edited in place. Changes made offline queue and send when you are back online.

What it does

  • Signs in to one or more Google accounts and shows all of their calendars in one view.
  • Creates, moves, copies and deletes events, RSVPs to invitations, and edits repeating series.
  • Keeps a local cache so the app opens instantly and works without a connection.

Download

Download Fork Calendar for Mac (v0.16.6, macOS 15 or later). The app checks for updates itself. Source and iPhone build instructions on GitHub.
