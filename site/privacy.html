Privacy policy

Last updated 25 September 2026

Summary

Fork Calendar runs entirely on your own Mac or iPhone. It has no server of its own. Your calendar data moves only between your device and Google.

What the app accesses

When you sign in, Fork Calendar asks Google for permission to read and write your Google Calendar (the calendar scope) and to read your email address and name so it can label the account. It requests nothing else.

How the data is used

  • Calendars and events are downloaded from Google and stored in a cache on your device so the app can show them and work offline.
  • Edits you make are sent back to Google Calendar, and only there.
  • Your email address and name are shown in the app's account list.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored on the device and are used only to talk to Google's Calendar API on your behalf.

What we do not do

  • No calendar data, tokens or account details are sent to CW&T or to any third party.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • No data is sold, shared or used to train models.

Deleting your data

Remove an account in the app's Settings to delete its cached data and tokens from the device, or delete the app. To withdraw Google's permission entirely, remove Fork Calendar at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services

Fork Calendar's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
